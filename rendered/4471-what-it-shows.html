Why subject matching missed it

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. The three problems share subjects such as "Wrong day on the invite", so they merged. Intake's intakeTag was no better: it tagged 15 of the 33 form-hint reports as parser.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs reported many times. Count by cause before you merge, not after.
  • A guess made at intake is not a cause. Check the tenant's locale and form.
