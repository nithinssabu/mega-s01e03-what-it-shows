#4471 · what it shows: two problems, not one

#4471 was merged as one issue with one fix queued. The reports in it describe two different problems. Fixing the first one will not fix the second.

Not one issue. Each problem below has its own ticket and its own count.

The two problems

412 reports merged into #4471 · 2 problems

problemreportsticket
parser reads month first379#1
booking-v3 form hint says MM/DD/YYYY33#2

5 of the parser reports read as a one-day move; counted under the parser by tenant.

  1. The parser reads dates month first. Tenants whose locale writes the day first get their dates flipped. This is the one #4471 describes, and the fix that is queued.
  2. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The date flips a second time. The parser fix does not touch it.

A report belongs to the cause of its tenant, its locale and its booking form. Neither what intake tagged nor how the reporter worded it decides that.

Why subject matching missed it

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. Both problems share subjects such as "Wrong day on the invite", so they merged. Intake's intakeTag was no better: it tagged 15 of the 33 form-hint reports as parser.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs reported many times. Count by cause before you merge, not after.
  • A guess made at intake is not a cause. Check the tenant's locale and form.
